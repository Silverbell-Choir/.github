# Code of Conduct

SilverBell Choir aims to keep project discussions productive, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas, code, behavior, or outcomes rather than people.
- Provide enough context for others to understand the issue or proposal.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, and deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's sensitive information.

## Moderation

Organization maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that materially disrupts collaboration or violates these expectations.

Repeated or severe violations may result in restricted participation.

## Scope

This applies to public collaboration spaces maintained by SilverBell Choir, including issues, pull requests, and discussions where enabled.
