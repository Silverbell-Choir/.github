## About

**SilverBell Choir** is an independent software organization for building and maintaining multiple products under a consistent structure.

Active development repositories may remain **private** when appropriate, while public support, security, and contribution guidance remain available through stable public repositories.

## How we work

- Each project is developed independently in its own repository.
- Public support is centralized in one shared support repository.
- Security, contribution, and collaboration guidance follows organization-wide defaults.
- The structure is designed to scale as new projects are added.
