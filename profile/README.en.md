## SilverBell Choir

**SilverBell Choir** is an independent software organization for building and maintaining multiple products under a consistent structure.

> [!NOTE]
> Development repositories may remain private, while public support, security, and contribution guidance remain available through shared public resources.

## Principles

- **Project isolation** — development scope and repositories remain independent by project.
- **Clear public/private boundaries** — public support and active development stay separated.
- **Stable support entry point** — users do not need to find a new support location whenever a project is added.
- **Organization-wide defaults** — shared security, contribution, and collaboration guidance is used where applicable.
